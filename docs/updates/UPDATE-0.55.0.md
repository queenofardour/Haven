# Haven 0.55.0: remodel the house itself

**Remodel.** While decorating a home (not a custom layout), the toolbar has a **Remodel** button next to Furnish. With it open, point at the house itself: a pillar, beam, wall, floor, stair, window, door or ceiling light. The piece under the mouse is outlined and named. Click it to choose it.
- **Remove** takes it away for everyone in your home. **Keep it solid** leaves it invisible but still walkable; it starts on for floors, stairs, terraces and ceilings.
- **Move it** swaps the piece for a Building piece of the same model, in the same spot, which you can then move, turn and scale like any furnishing.
- The panel lists everything you removed, with **Put it back** and **Put everything back**. Undo works too.
- The game builds each floor's walls, its floor and its ceiling as one piece each, so those come off a whole floor at a time.
- If a removed piece leaves a hole and you fall well below the lowest floor, you're brought back to where you last stood.

**Building pieces in every home.** Furnish now offers Building pieces (walls, floors, pillars, stairs, windows, doors and lights from every district) in any home, not just custom layouts, so you can rebuild what you remove.

Your changes to the house travel with the home: guests see them, and saved layouts keep them.

This update comes with relay 0.38.0 (housing protocol 34). Older Haven versions can't join gatherings on the new relay, so everyone needs to update.
