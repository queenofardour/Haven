# Haven 0.52.6: a starting Enhancement, and Sudden Death keeps your cards in the Odyssey

**In the Crystal Odyssey, Sudden Death no longer hands your cards to the opponent.** In ordinary Triple Triad, a drawn match is dealt again with each side holding the cards it owned at the end, captured ones included. So a replay could start with one of your upgraded cards in the opponent's hand, playing against you, while you held one of theirs. In the Odyssey's duels, Sudden Death now deals each side the five cards it started the duel with. Who goes first is unchanged: whoever still had a card in hand.

The Swap rule is unchanged: under Swap (Gilgamesh, and some duels from World IX), one card from each hand is still traded before play, and a Sudden Death replay keeps that trade. Your cards were never lost after a duel; this only changes who plays them during a replay.

Ordinary Triple Triad on the Board, against NPCs or friends, keeps the game's own Sudden Death.

**A gift for the road.** Once you accept your starting binder (no more re-deals), the Odyssey offers you a free Enhancement before World I: three Common Enhancements to choose from, no Materia or Items, and no reroll. Choose one and put it on a card, or skip it. Runs could end in the first duel or two on bad luck alone, before they had anything to work with.

**Prism Powder no longer hides one of the opponent's cards.** When the opponent held an exact copy of one of your unupgraded cards, Prism Powder hid their copy along with yours. Their copy now stays shown. Found while checking the gift.
