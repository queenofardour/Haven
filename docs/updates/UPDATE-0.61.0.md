# Haven 0.61.0: who controls what in a gathering

**Your NPCs are yours.** Only you and the guests you let decorate can possess your home's NPCs. Other guests see the Possess button greyed out, and every PC in the gathering ignores anyone else trying to take one. If you stop someone decorating while they hold an NPC, they let go of it.

**Pose balls.** Hiding a set's balls (which stands everyone on them up) is now up to you, your decorators, and whoever is on one of that set's balls or has the set open. Others no longer see the button.

**Copies of a home have their own NPCs.** Duplicating a home, importing one, or saving a copy of a friend's home gives the copy's NPCs new identities. They start with the same mods the originals have on your PC, and from then on changes to one don't touch the other.

**Approving decorators' work.** A decorator's staged changes all reach you now, up to 128 per decorator and 512 per home. Before, a big group move could leave changes you never saw.

**Names in room chat.** A line in room chat carries the name of the character who said it, as your game shows that character in the home. A character's name in a gathering is also the one it was first signed in with on its Discord account.

**Relay limits per Discord account.** Signed-in players are limited per account rather than per internet connection, so several people sharing one connection no longer share one allowance. This doesn't change anything in normal play.

This update comes with relay 0.42.0. Haven 0.60.x keeps working with it.
