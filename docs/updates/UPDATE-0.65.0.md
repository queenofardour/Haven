# Haven 0.65.0: everything in the game's world, as furniture

**Furnish → Catalogue → World.**
- Every model the game places in its world is now a piece you can place: lanterns from Kugane, Ishgard's railings, Gridania's trees, the crates of a dungeon, the Gold Saucer's signs, a Dawntrail rock. Over a hundred thousand of them, in every zone your game has.
- Each has a picture drawn from its own model and textures, and a name for what it is ("Weapon rack", "Market stall awning", "Brass scales"; variants of one piece are numbered). Search by name, zone or file, and narrow the list by kind (architecture, furnishings, lighting, nature) and by zone.
- Click one to place it, as a building piece: it moves, turns, scales, undoes and saves like any other, and guests in a gathering see it.
- The first time you open World, Haven lists the game's world from your own game data, which takes a few seconds; after that it's kept, and listed again only after a game patch. Pictures are drawn as you scroll, the first time each one is shown.

**Good to know.**
- Many of these pieces are large (a cliff, a whole wall of a town) and most aren't solid to walk on: the game makes them solid only where its own collision says so, and Haven keeps exactly that.
- A piece's lights, effects and sounds aren't placed with it yet; its model, textures and collision are.
- A guest sees a world piece only when their own game has it: on a free trial, pieces from Endwalker and Dawntrail aren't drawn for them (nothing breaks; they just aren't there).
- The names were given by looking at each piece's picture, about 28,600 of them, so a few will be off, and the smallest or flattest pieces have plain names ("Wall section", "Terrain piece"). A piece without one is named from where the game uses it ("Kugane lantern 3").

Everyone in a gathering needs Haven 0.65.0, and the relay is updated with it.
