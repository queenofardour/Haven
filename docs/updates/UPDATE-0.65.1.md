# Haven 0.65.1: the World catalogue, cleaned up

**Pieces that showed nothing are gone from the catalogue.** About 3,700 of the game's world models hold nothing you can see: only the glow of a sunbeam, a fog volume or a shadow. They had no picture in Furnish → World and set down as an invisible square (the Future Rewritten "floors", many "landscape" and "piece" entries). They're no longer listed.

**Groups the game builds from parts can be placed.** Sets such as the Alexandria airship said they couldn't be put in the game: their moving parts are authored past the limits Haven checks furniture against. About 650 such sets now place, with any motion Haven can't follow left still.

**Better names.** Many files the game numbers as one family are really different objects: the airship, its propeller, fences, planters and pipes in one Alexandria folder were all "Black stone pillar". Every piece that looks different from its family's picture now has a name of its own, from its own picture (about 57,000 pieces). A set's name ends in "(set)", so the assembled airship and its separate parts are told apart.

Client only: the relay is unchanged.
