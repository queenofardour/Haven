# Haven 0.55.2: walls you can choose

**Walls can be chosen in Remodel.** Pointing at a wall used to choose the pillars and beams in front of it, even ones you had already removed. Haven now asks the game which piece your pointer actually hits, so the wall is the wall. Windows, doors and ceiling lights are still chosen where you point at them.

**Take the pillars with the walls.** With a floor's walls (or its ceiling and structure) chosen, the Remodel panel offers **Remove it with the N pillars and beams on it**: everything standing along it comes off in one step, and one undo puts it all back.

**The ring and the rafters.** A floor's ceiling and structure piece, which also holds the ring under a pillar and the rafters below the floor, can now be chosen near its bottom as well as its top and sides.

This update needs no relay change: everyone on 0.55.x can play with you.
