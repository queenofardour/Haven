# Haven 0.53.3: NPCs get their skeletons

**NPCs on pose balls, for real this time.** 0.53.1 was meant to give your NPCs the skeleton files from your Penumbra collection, so animations made for a modded skeleton fit them. Haven asked Penumbra for it by the wrong names, so it never happened and NPCs still T-posed. Fixed: NPCs now get your skeletons when they appear, and if Penumbra isn't ready yet, Haven asks again for the next NPC.

Haven's log says what it did: "NPCs are drawn with N skeleton file(s) from your Penumbra collection".

This update needs no relay change: everyone on 0.53.x can play with you.
