# Haven 0.64.2: the weather heard outside, played the game's way

**Heard outside works without breaking the music.** In 0.64.0 and 0.64.1, choosing a weather under Room → Atmosphere → Heard outside never played it: Haven asked the game's music player for it, and that player can't take a second track while the home's music is playing. The request stalled, the home's music stopped working (it wouldn't start again once stopped), and the real-world zone you came from could be heard instead, as a drone or its own ambience. Haven now plays the weather the way the game plays its own weather sounds, on its own sound player beside the music, using the game's files as they are (no conversion, and Penumbra isn't needed).

If your music player is still stuck from trying 0.64.0 or 0.64.1, restart the game once.

Also still in: the day's or night's sound changes once a dragged time has settled for a second, and a sound that keeps stopping is left off for a few minutes rather than started over and over.

Client only: the relay is unchanged.
