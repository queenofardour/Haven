# Haven 0.56.4: NPCs wear their own mods

**Give an NPC its own mods.** In the NPCs panel's new Mods section, choose a Penumbra collection for the NPC and press **Share mods for this NPC** (it needs a Glamourer look). For a moment you wear the NPC's collection and look, faded out, so your guests' sync plugins (Lightless, PlayerSync, Snowcloak) pick up its mods; then your own look comes back. Haven never uploads the files: each PC keeps the ones its sync plugin fetched, and the NPC wears them on later visits too, without sharing again.

Guests who aren't paired with you in a sync plugin see the NPC's plain look. **Finish sharing** ends a share early, and **Forget its mods here** drops them on your PC.

This update needs no relay change: everyone on 0.56.x can play with you. Guests on an older version see the NPC's plain look.
