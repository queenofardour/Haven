# Haven 0.58.4: inn fixes

**Inns load straight away.** Entering an inn room held the loading screen for a full minute; it now lifts as soon as the room is ready. Friends in a gathering and your NPCs no longer wait on it either.

**Remodelling an inn works properly.** Point at any piece of the room, however small, and it's the one chosen: pots, lamps, books and ornaments included. The room's walls and other large parts are called "Room structure", and the panel explains that an inn's pieces can be removed and put back, but not moved. Walls and floors stay solid when removed unless you say otherwise.

Some inns build their furniture into the room itself: in the Hourglass the bed, armoire and chairs are part of the room's structure, so they come off only with it.

Also fixed: a garbled character in Remodel's pointer label.

This update needs no relay change: everyone on 0.57.x and 0.58.x can play with you. Friends need 0.58.3 or later to join you in an inn.
