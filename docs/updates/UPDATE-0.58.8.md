# Haven 0.58.8: NPC mods reach your guests

**Sending an NPC's mods to guests now gives them the files.** Your sync plugin only passes on files kept in Penumbra's mod folder, and Haven was dressing you from its own copies elsewhere, so your guests got the NPC's outfit with none of its mods. Haven now puts the NPC's files in a Penumbra mod of its own first ("<name> (Haven NPC)", under Haven NPC Mods) and dresses you from there. Your guests should see their sync plugin's download bar above you while the files arrive. The mod changes nothing if you switch it on yourself; Forget its mods removes it.

**Turn Glamourer automation off while you share.** If your automation puts your own look back, the share stops and says so: switch automation off for your character in Glamourer while you share, then send again.

Also from 0.58.7, now confirmed: friends show as soon as they join.

This update needs no relay change: everyone on 0.57.x and 0.58.x can play with you.
