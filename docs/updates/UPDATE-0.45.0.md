# Haven 0.45.0: aquariums, vase flowers, fish prints, Triple Triad, notices and What's new

**Furnishings**
- **Aquariums hold fish.** Select a tank in decorating and use Add fish… in its inspector. Fish swim with their own animation, the water matches them (fresh or salt), and Decoration picks one of six arrangements. Tanks follow the game's rules: a tier N tank has N places, small fish take 1, medium 2, large 3 and extra large 4, and all fish share one water.
- **Vases hold flowers.** Choose them in the vase's inspector, from every flower the game has. Their colour isn't applied yet: the game sets it at runtime, and we still have to find how.
- **Angler's Canvases show fish prints.** Choose the fish in the canvas's inspector; each canvas size lists the prints of its size.
- **Triple Triad at the Triple Triad Board.** Click the board to play Triple Triad against Haven with the standard rules. Your hand is five of the cards you own, Haven's matches its stars, and nothing is won or lost.

**Notices and updates**
- **What's new.** After an update, Haven shows the notes for every version since the one you last saw. Open them any time with `/haven changelog` or Settings → General → What's new.
- **Notices from the relay.** The operator can announce something to everyone in a gathering: a new version, or relay maintenance with a countdown. Maintenance shows a countdown under the room's toolbar, and your gathering reconnects by itself after the restart. Notices also appear as a notification and a chat line.
- **Haven tells you when there's an update**, whatever your Dalamud auto-update setting. Dalamud's own "Updates available" notice leaves out custom-repository plugins when it is set to "Auto-update main repository plugins"; "Only notify me" or "Auto-update all plugins" include Haven. Haven now checks its own feed every hour and notifies you once per new version. Click the notification to open the installer.
- **Dalamud's installer shows Haven's notes** in its Changelogs tab.

Shared visits need Haven 0.45.0 and relay 0.30.0 (housing protocol 28). Not yet tried in game.
