# Haven 0.49.4: a first test of NPCs

**Try an NPC (test version).** In a home on your own, type `/haven npc`: an NPC wearing a copy of your look appears in front of you. Type `/haven possess` to control it: the camera follows the NPC, your own character stays exactly where it is (seated, emoting, whatever it was doing), and WASD walks the NPC around (hold Ctrl to walk slowly). It stops at walls and furniture. `/haven possess` again lets go; `/haven npc` again removes it, and leaving the home removes it too. A small bar under the toolbar has the same buttons.

This is an early test: NPCs aren't saved, guests can't see them yet, and mods on your look may not carry over to the NPC. Please report how it looks and moves.

Client only: works with relay 0.32.0.
