# Haven 0.49.1: a clearer Crystal Odyssey

**Bigger, sharper Odyssey screens.** The Odyssey now scales to your screen, so cards, their numbers and text are easier to read. The binder, the opponent's hand, claimed cards and packs show their cards larger. The game's fonts are drawn at their own sizes, so text in Haven's game windows (Triple Triad included) is sharper. Small text no longer has a ragged outline.

**Plain words.** Perks say what they do. The Crown, for example: "If you own this card when the duel ends, it counts as two cards." The run's currency is gil.

Client only: works with relay 0.32.0.
