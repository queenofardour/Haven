# Haven 0.58.5: gathering fixes

**Friends appear when they join.** A friend joining your gathering, and you on their screen, could stay invisible until someone redrew them in Penumbra. Haven now redraws each person a moment after they join.

**Sharing an NPC's mods works with one guest.** Send its mods to guests stayed greyed out ("Host a gathering first") unless two or more friends were in the home.

**Remodelling an inn, continued.** Removing a lamp now takes its light with it, and removing a bed takes away the part you'd bump into, unless you choose to keep it solid. The Unending Journey's book can be removed too, and pieces holding an inn object are named after it: Bed, Armoire, Glamour dresser and so on.

This update needs no relay change: everyone on 0.57.x and 0.58.x can play with you.
