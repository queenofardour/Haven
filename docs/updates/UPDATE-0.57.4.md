# Haven 0.57.4: NPC mods keep their gear

**Sharing an NPC's mods waits for your new look.** When you share an NPC's mods, Haven briefly dresses you as the NPC. A change of race takes a few moments to finish, and Haven used to read your look before your gear had loaded. The NPC then kept a body mod but not its gear, and could lose its hands and feet. Haven now waits until your look has settled before it reads it.

If you shared an NPC's mods on an earlier version and it looks broken, share them again.

This update needs no relay change: everyone on 0.57.x can play with you.
