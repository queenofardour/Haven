# Haven 0.56.3: doors you can keep shut

**Turn off a furnishing's walk-up door.** Furnishings with a door that opens as you walk up to it (the Trick Bookshelf Partition's hidden door, the partition doors, the Swinging-door Counter) now have a **Door opens** switch in the inspector. Turn it off and the door stays shut and solid, so a bookshelf is just a bookshelf. Like a lamp's Lit switch, it's remembered with your home and shared with everyone in your gathering.

This update needs no relay change: everyone on 0.56.x can play with you. Guests on an older version see the door open as before.
