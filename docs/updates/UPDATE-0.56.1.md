# Haven 0.56.1: finishes that stay, tidier NPC levels

**Walls, floors and lights that stay put.** On some PCs, straight after starting the game, a home's walls, floors or ceiling lights could show the shell's defaults even though the Room panel showed your choices. Choosing them again fixed it until the next restart. Haven now sends a home's finishes, and its brightness, once more a few seconds after you arrive, when everything has loaded.

**NPC levels.** An NPC's level no longer shows above its head; like a player's, it shows only when you target it, now with a gap between the level and the name.

This update needs no relay change: everyone on 0.56.x can play with you.
