# Haven 0.64.3: no real-world ambience inside a home

**The zone you came from can no longer be heard inside a home.** Inside a home you could still hear the ambience of the real place you left: Kugane's wind and gulls, a drone from a Cosmic Exploration zone. Real homes have no such sound. Haven now quiets it on entering and puts it back once you're home again. The home's music and the weather heard outside are unaffected.

Client only: the relay is unchanged.
