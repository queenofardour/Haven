# Haven 0.46.1: Triple Triad as the game plays it, and personal bests

**Machine names.** Cuff-a-Cur and The Moogle's Paw asked to play each other's game. Each machine's prompt now names its own game.

**No ticket choice.** The prompt no longer offers "Use one of five Gold Saucer tickets": Haven's games are free.

**Easier to aim at.** The machines are several metres tall, so their outline and label covered the whole cabinet, high above you. Now only the part you play at answers the cursor and lights up, with the label just above it:
- Cuff-a-Cur: Gilgamesh;
- Crystal Tower Striker: the hammers and the target;
- Monster Toss: the hoop and the posts;
- The Moogle's Paw: the control panel and the claw;
- The Finer Miner: the ore;
- Out on a Limb: the log.

**Personal bests.** Each machine remembers your best on this PC, per character, and nothing is stored anywhere else:
- Monster Toss counts baskets;
- Cuff-a-Cur and the Crystal Tower Striker keep your best hit (up to BRUTAL!!! and PULVERIZING!!!);
- The Moogle's Paw keeps your best catch;
- Out on a Limb and The Finer Miner keep the points you bank, at the rates the Gold Saucer pays MGP.

The prompt shows your best ("Play a round of Cuff-a-Cur? Your personal best: PUNISHING!!"), chat tells you after each game, and Double Down speaks of points, not MGP.

**Triple Triad, much closer to the game.**
- **Your decks.** Choose one of the decks you built in the game's Triple Triad Decks window, or play with five of your own cards.
- **The end of a match** shows the game's result window: You Win!, You Lose... or Draw, with Rematch and Quit. Matches no longer start again by themselves.
- **Leaving.** Escape during a match asks whether to leave (Quit, or Keep playing).
- **Cards move as in the game.** The hands are dealt in at the start, a dragged card follows the pointer and drops onto the board, and captured cards turn over and change colour halfway.
- **The turn timer** runs down under each name, 15 seconds a turn, with the game's warning tick in the last 5. When time runs out, a card is played for you.
- **Sounds.** The card sounds use the game's own Triple Triad sounds (the capture sound was the wrong one), with the turn change and the opening, START! and winning jingles. They're played on the game's own thread now; if a match is still silent, the log names each sound as it is asked for (`[GAMES] UI sound …`).
- **Music** plays again on later matches (it stopped after you left a home mid-match), and the home's music comes back however the match ends.
- **The opponent picker** has one close button and two tabs, Game players and Haven, so choosing Haven's rules no longer squeezes the list.

Client only: works with relay 0.30.0.
