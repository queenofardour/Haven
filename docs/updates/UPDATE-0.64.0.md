# Haven 0.64.0: moods, rain outside, copies in rows and circles, your own groups, and /random

**Moods and atmosphere** (Room panel → Atmosphere).
- **Hold a time of day:** pick an Eorzean time for the home, in quarter hours; your furniture clocks show it too. Off, the home follows Eorzea's clock as before.
- **Heard outside:** rain, dripping rain, a rainstorm, a thunderstorm, wind, snow or a blizzard, from the game's own weather sounds, playing quietly as if through the walls (day and night versions follow the home's time). Each person sets its volume for themselves; it plays with your music volume.
- **Weather:** rooms whose level holds several weathers (most inns) can choose one. Housing interiors hold only Fair Skies in the game's data, so they don't offer a choice: the rain is heard rather than seen.
- **Moods:** Save as a mood… keeps the brightness, time of day, weather, what's heard outside and, if you like, the music, under a name ("Evening", "Rainy afternoon"). Click a mood to switch to it; right-click to rename, update or delete it. A home keeps up to 12, and guests in a gathering see the change.
- Original interior and Apply layout leave your moods and atmosphere alone.

**Copies** (in the inspector of a piece or a selection).
- **In a row:** copies a set distance apart, along X, Z or stacked, with a count.
- **In a circle:** copies spaced round a centre in front of the piece, each turned toward it, so a chair facing its table becomes chairs round the table.
- **Mirrored:** a mirror image across X or Z, beside the pieces or in place, keeping the originals or flipping them. Pieces aren't flipped themselves (the game draws them as made), so one that differs left and right keeps its own hand.
- The copies show where they'll stand while you adjust them; set them down with a click, a right-click, Set down or Done; Escape or Cancel throws them away. Copies of a linked group are linked groups of their own.

**Your groups.**
- **Keep in Your groups…** in the inspector saves a piece or a selection, dyes, pictures and pose balls included, to your own library on this PC, for all your characters.
- Find them under Furnish → Yours → Your groups: click one to place it (it follows the cursor like a new piece), right-click to rename or delete it. Settings → Files opens the folder.

**/random and /dice in a home.**
- Rolls show in the room as the game shows its own ("Random! You roll a 512.", or "(out of 20)" with an upper limit from 2 to 999). The relay rolls the number, so everyone sees the same roll and nobody can pick theirs.

Everyone in a gathering needs Haven 0.64.0, and the relay is updated with it.
