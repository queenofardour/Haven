# Haven 0.59.0: NPC mods stay in step

**Your guests follow your NPCs' current mods.** When you forget an NPC's mods, your guests see it without them too, straight away or when they next join. If a guest has an older version of an NPC's mods than yours, you're told ("… has older mods for …") so you can send them again; the NPC's Mods tab counts who still has the old version.

**Friends show after a visit ends.** A friend who stayed invisible once the gathering ended (their party list showing you in another zone) is now drawn again automatically within a few seconds.

This update needs no relay change: everyone on 0.57.x and 0.58.x can play with you. Both you and your guests need 0.59.0 to keep NPC mods in step.
