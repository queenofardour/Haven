# Haven 0.61.1: when a guest's game can't load your home

**You're told why a guest left.** If someone joins your gathering but their game doesn't have the expansion your home needs (a free trial joining an Endwalker or Dawntrail inn), they leave straight away, as before, and now you get a message saying who tried to join and which expansion the home needs.

**They're no longer invisible afterwards.** A guest who joined and left at once could stay invisible to you after you left your home. Haven now shows everyone who was in the gathering again when you return, and redraws anyone who still isn't shown.

This update comes with relay 0.42.1. Haven 0.60.x and 0.61.0 keep working with it; the message about a guest who left needs 0.61.1 on both sides.
