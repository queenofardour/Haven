# Haven 0.52.5: NPC colours in Glamourer, and two Odyssey improvements

**Changing an NPC's colours in Glamourer works.** Skin, hair, highlights, eyes, lips, tattoo and face paint colours did nothing on an NPC, even after sending it away and back. Gear dyes and Glamourer's Advanced Customization still worked. When Haven saves an NPC's Glamourer look (Copy my look, a Glamourer design, or Save Glamourer edits), Glamourer hands it every Advanced Customization colour marked as set, even the ones nobody changed. Those colours override the ordinary ones, so the ordinary colour settings had no effect.

Haven now tidies a look the first time it dresses an NPC in it. It lets the advanced colours go for a moment, sees which colours the game draws from the ordinary settings, and keeps only the advanced colours that really differ, as Glamourer's own copy does. Advanced colours you set on purpose are kept. If you may decorate the home, the tidied look is saved, so this happens once. The NPC may flicker briefly while it is tidied.

NPCs you already have are tidied the next time they appear.

**The Crystal Odyssey:**
- **Fourteen new Enhancements and fourteen new Items.** There were 41 Materia but only 13 Enhancements and 10 Items, so the same few kept coming back. Enhancements now go from 13 to 27 and Items from 10 to 24, offered a little more evenly (Enhancement 36, Materia 34, Item 30).
  - **Enhancements:** reshaping ones (Mythril Whetstone: +2 to one side and -1 to the opposite; Fantasia: swap two sides) and new card traits:
    - Vanguard, Desperation, Keystone, Blizzard, Banner and Drain;
    - the Looking Glass: the card takes with *lower* numbers;
    - the Masquerade: the card counts a star lower for deck limits;
    - two-trait scrolls (Bastion, Rampage) and two Legendaries: Adamant (the card can never be taken) and the Mimic (it copies the higher numbers it faces).
  - **Items:**
    - counters to specific threats: Holy Water for Reverse, Tranquilizer for Order and Chaos, and Shell Stone, which stops Same, Plus and combos from taking your cards;
    - Bomb Fragment, Prism Powder (hide your hand), Arctic Wind, Hero Drink and Protect Stone;
    - the Card Club Rulebook, Treasure Maps for better rewards, and the Queen of Cards' Favour (you add a rule);
    - two Legendaries: the Ribbon (only the open-hand rule and Sudden Death apply, and their Materia is cancelled) and Pandora's Box (a random Legendary Materia for one duel).
  - Late opponents can now carry Keystone, Blizzard, Drain and Desperation too. The Wandering Merchant sells nothing above Rare.
- **Smoke Bomb has a proper icon** (it showed a blank circle).
- **Libra scans your opponents.** On the Choose Your Opponent screen, Libra now shows each opponent's whole hand, so you can pick your duel knowing what they hold. Before, it only showed the opponent's hand in duels, and in Worlds I to V every duel already does that (All Open), so it did nothing there. It still shows their whole hand in duels under Three Open.
- **A victory fanfare after every won duel.** As the win shows, a fanfare plays: FFXIV's "Victory!", or FINAL FANTASY IX's and XII's own fanfares in their worlds. Then the world's theme takes over for the reward screen.
- **No more Triple Triad jingles in Odyssey duels.** Duels started Triple Triad's own opening and win jingles over the Odyssey's music, and so did opening the Odyssey.
- **New music, chosen by hand.** Most worlds have new tracks for their screens, duels, elites and bosses, such as The Seventh Sun and Freshly Glazed Porxie for World I, The Chase/Crazy Motorcycle and Let the Battles Begin! for World VII, and Blinded by Light and Saber's Edge for World XIII. The Odyssey can now play any track in the game, not only orchestrion rolls.
- **Each world has its own theme, and duels play battle music.** The screens between duels used to play each world's duel track, which for half the worlds was a battle theme, so World II played battle music before any duel. Now every world has a theme for its screens (such as The Red Wings for World IV and Shuffle or Boogie for World VIII) and a battle track for its duels. 0.52.3's change, which moved regular duels to the elite track, is undone. In World II that had given regular duels "The Emperor's Wont" instead of the battle theme.
- **More Materia slots.** Every boss now offers a Materia Slot beside its Rare Materia while you have fewer than six. Before, a slot was one Rare reward among a dozen, so most runs stayed on three slots for a long time.
- **More information on hover.**
  - A card's tooltip lists what was done to it: each upgraded side (such as "Top 5 → 6"), a changed type and what that means, and its perk with what it does.
  - In duels, hovering a rule in the top right explains it, and hovering a card (yours, the board's, or theirs once it's face up) shows its type and perk.
  - Your own Materia now show beside your name, like the opponent's do beside theirs, and hovering any Materia explains it.
- **The shop warns you when your Materia slots are full.** Buying a Materia with no free slot asks you to replace one of yours (or let the new one go). The purchase question now says so before you spend the gil.
