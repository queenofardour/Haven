# Haven 0.60.1: the Crystal Odyssey's World IX, and a round of fixes

**World IX no longer crashes the game.** Playing the Crystal Odyssey in World IX could freeze the game and close it with "Could not locate data files" (c0500). The world's theme, "Thy Warmth", is a short piece without a loop, and the game's music player can't cope with a song that runs out. Haven now starts such a song again just before it ends, so Thy Warmth simply repeats. The victory fanfare after a won duel also hands over a moment earlier, before it ends.

**Your look after sharing an NPC's mods.** After a share, your own look is put back without Glamourer holding it in place, so changing gearset or glamour shows straight away.

**NPC mods stay with your guests.** Guests no longer lose an NPC's kept mods when you host from another PC, after reinstalling, or when someone hosts a copy of your home. Only pressing Forget takes them away.

**Inn rooms catch you if you fall.** Removing an inn's floor and stepping where it was brings you back to where you last stood, as in a house. Tilted inn pieces show as removable only, instead of offering Move it and then refusing.

**Safer and tidier.** Leaving a home and unloading Haven at the same moment can no longer leave the game believing you're still inside. An NPC Copy my look too large to carry is refused instead of kept broken, and some smaller fixes keep NPC names, animations and server deletes in order.

This update needs no relay change: everyone on 0.60.0 can play with you.
