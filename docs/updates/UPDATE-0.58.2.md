# Haven 0.58.2: NPC mod counts

**Every NPC's row shows its mods.** NPCs whose mods were kept before 0.58.1 showed only "mods" in their row. Haven now counts them itself, once per home, by finding their files in your Penumbra mods folder, so the row reads "2 mods", "7 mods" and so on. Sending an NPC's mods to guests again keeps its count too.

A guest's NPCs, whose files came from a sync plugin, still show "mods" until the owner copies or sends them again.

This update needs no relay change: everyone on 0.57.x and 0.58.x can play with you.
