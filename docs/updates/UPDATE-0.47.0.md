# Haven 0.47.0: Triple Triad with another player

**Play someone in your gathering.** At the Triple Triad Board, the new **Players here** tab lists everyone in the gathering. Choose someone and press Invite. They get a challenge on their screen with your rules, pick their own deck, and accept or decline. You each see your own cards in blue on the left, as in the game. Moves are timed as usual, and Rematch works once you both ask for it. If one of you leaves, the other is told. The rules are the ones picked on the Haven tab.

**Triple Triad fixes.**
- **The turn clock** shows under each name again (seconds and hundredths, as in the game). The names moved to the band beneath the bar, where the game puts them.
- **The start of a match** plays in order: the board comes in first, then the cards are dealt, then START!. Before, all three ran at once and the board jumped.
- **The result window** no longer shows blank for a few seconds the first time.
- **The opponent picker** always leaves room for its Play button.

**Other windows after a match.** Finishing a game left every Dalamud window drawn after it (Chat 2, for one) without its padding until the game restarted. Fixed; if your windows still look cramped from 0.46.x, restart the game once. The game's window now comes to the front while you play, so clicks on your cards no longer drag the chat window underneath.

Needs relay 0.31.0 for two-player matches; everything else works with relay 0.30.0.
