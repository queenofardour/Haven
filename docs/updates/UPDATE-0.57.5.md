# Haven 0.57.5: sharing NPC mods with Glamourer automation

**Your NPC's gear comes through.** If you use Glamourer automation, sharing an NPC's mods could put your own clothes back on you mid-share, so the NPC's gear mods never got captured. The NPC then came out half-dressed or broken. Haven now dresses you in the NPC's look again after the change settles, before it reads your look.

If an NPC looks broken from an earlier share, share its mods again.

This update needs no relay change: everyone on 0.57.x can play with you.
