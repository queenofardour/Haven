# Haven 0.48.0: flower colours, machines that react, and the Ale Tap pours

**Vase flowers in their own colour.** Blue Oldroses are blue, yellow are yellow: each colour of a flower is its own model in the game, and Haven now uses the right one for every flower (all 376 choices).

**Arcade machines react as in the Gold Saucer.**
- Their lamps go dark on a hit and light up again, or stay dark on a miss.
- Monster Toss's ball flies on every throw.
- Out on a Limb and The Finer Miner flash red on a miss.

**Others in your gathering see you play.** They see the machine react to your result, and you take your stance at the machine and swing or punch. The result balloons (BRUTAL!!! and so on) still show only to the player.

**Furnishings you use.** The Ale Tap pours when clicked: the lever moves, the ale and its sound flow, and the lamp blinks while it pours. The Projection Module, the Purification Module and the Syrcus Tower play their effect the same way. Everyone in the gathering sees it.

**Triple Triad's end and leave windows** look the part: a heading in the game's lettering (You Win!, You Lose..., Draw, or Leave the Match?), a full-size line beneath it, and clearer button labels.

Needs relay 0.32.0 for others to see machines and uses; everything else works with relay 0.31.0.
