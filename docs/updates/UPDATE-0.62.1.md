# Haven 0.62.1: invitations, your NPCs on visits, and a tougher Odyssey

**Friends nearby are invited to your gathering.**
- While you host, any friend with Haven who is standing near you gets a prompt: "<you> is hosting a gathering in <home>. Would you like to join?" They can Join or Decline.
- If they decline, that gathering doesn't ask them again. The visit code still works as before.
- The Visit panel has a new Invitations section:
  - **Invite friends nearby automatically**, on by default;
  - an **Invite** button for each friend nearby;
  - **Only people I invite can join**, which turns away everyone else, even someone who has the code.
- Your friends need Haven 0.62.1 and must be signed in to Haven.

**Your NPCs come along.**
- Save an NPC from a home you own or decorate to **Your NPCs**, a new view in the NPCs panel. Each character has its own list on the PC.
- **Add to this home** copies a saved NPC into another of your homes, mods included.
- **Bring it along** brings a saved NPC into a friend's gathering as a visitor. It is never saved into their home.
  - It wears its Glamourer look, its Customize+ profile and, once you share them, its Penumbra mods.
  - You choose who may possess it: only you, you and the home's owner, or anyone there.
  - The home's owner can send any visitor away, or turn visitors away altogether.
  - Up to two visitors per guest, four per home.
- Everyone in the gathering needs Haven 0.62.1 to see visitors.

**The Crystal Odyssey:**
- **At most three Enhancements on a card.** Card tooltips show how many a card has taken.
- **One Legendary Materia at a time.** Gaining a second asks you to replace the one you have, or let the new one go.
- **Master Materia:** a very rare Legendary, found at most once a run, that lets you slot a second Legendary.
- **Sell a card at the shop:** one per visit. A card sells for 8 to 40 gil by its stars, plus 10 gil for each Enhancement. You can't sell a card you'd need to make a deck.
- **The shop** now offers two Enhancements and three Items.
- **Sudden Death is no longer listed.** Every Odyssey duel plays it, so it no longer takes a line in the rule box; the deck screen says once that a draw is played again.

**Settings is tidier.**
- Stagehand capture and Linked Stagehand homes are gone. Homes captured before are ordinary homes and stay as they are.
- The footer now shows only messages from what you did in Settings.

This update comes with relay 0.43.0. Haven 0.60.x and 0.61.x keep working with it, without invitations, the invite-only lock or visiting NPCs.
