# Haven 0.50.0: NPCs in your home

**NPCs are saved in the home and everyone sees them.** Open the Room panel's new **NPCs** section and choose **Add NPC**: an NPC in your current look (weapons included) appears in front of you. Each one can be renamed, given a fresh copy of your look, saved where it stands, or removed, and every change is one undo step. A home holds up to eight. Guests see them in their saved look; decorators' changes are staged for the owner like furnishings.

**Possess an NPC** with **Possess** beside it (or `/haven possess` for the nearest). The camera follows the NPC while your own character stays where it is, seated or emoting. WASD walks it (Ctrl walks slowly), **Space jumps**, and it falls off ledges. **Let go** leaves it where you left it for the visit.

**Act as the NPC.** While possessing, typed emotes such as `/bow` or `/sit` play on the NPC, and `/say` lines appear as the NPC's in everyone's chat. Turn **Say as NPC** off on the bar to speak as yourself.

**In a gathering** friends see the NPC walk, jump, emote and speak. Only one person can possess an NPC at a time.

Not yet: NPCs on pose balls, the emote window playing on the NPC, and modded looks.

Also: the loading screen counts furnishings rather than their models.

Needs relay 0.33.0 (housing protocol 29). Older Haven versions can't join gatherings on it, so everyone should update.
