# Haven 0.65.5: tidier storage

**Downloads for other people's homes no longer pile up.** The pictures, furniture and animations Haven downloads when you visit a friend's home are now kept up to a limit: 2 GB unless you choose otherwise. Past it, what a home used longest ago is cleared when Haven starts, and downloaded again on your next visit to that home. Your own content is never cleared, and neither is anything a home or saved group on your PC uses (a copy of a friend's home included).

Settings → Custom content → **On this PC** shows how much is kept and lets you choose the limit (500 MB to 10 GB). **Clear downloaded content…** clears it all at once. Both wait until you're outside a home.

**Picture frames tidy themselves.** Each frame's picture is built on your PC from its original. Built frames that no home shows any more are now deleted when Haven starts. They are rebuilt in a moment if needed.

**NPC mods in Settings.** Settings → Custom content → **NPC mods** lists the mods your PC keeps for NPCs: yours, those of Your NPCs, and those friends sent you for theirs. You can see each one's size and where it's used, and remove those no home on your PC uses. Removing them there doesn't tell anyone else. A friend can send their NPC's mods again.

**Fixed:** a song downloaded for a copy of a friend's home was deleted at the next start if it played only in one of that home's moods.

This release changes Haven only; the relay is unchanged.
