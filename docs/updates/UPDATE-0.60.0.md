# Haven 0.60.0: move inn furniture, layouts keep what's on show

**Inn furniture moves.** In an inn room's Remodel, pieces now offer Move it, just as in a house: beds, armoires, chairs, lamps, The Unending Journey and the rest. Put it back returns a piece to its place. A few small items and windows the game sets at a tilt can still only be removed, and a moved lamp leaves its light behind, switched off.

**Saved layouts keep what's on show.** Saving a real house's layout now keeps its paintings, partition wallpapers, Angler's Canvas prints, vase flowers (in their colour) and aquarium fish. If anything is missing, the Dalamud log's `[LAYOUT]` lines say what was read.

**Storage in plain units.** Haven's shared storage shows as the 10 GB it is (it read 9.31 GB), and your own allowance as 250 MB.

**Your other characters are named.** A character who first reached Haven's server by uploading content or checking the allowance now sends their name too.

This update comes with relay 0.41.0: everyone in a gathering needs Haven 0.60.0.
