# Haven 0.53.1: NPCs dance, bosses look the part

**NPCs play their pose ball animations.** An NPC on a pose ball stood in a T-pose instead of playing its animation. Many animation mods are made for a modded skeleton with extra bones, and your NPCs were drawn with Penumbra's Default collection, which doesn't have it.
- Each visit, Haven now gives your NPCs the skeleton files from your own Penumbra collection, and nothing else of it. Your body and other cosmetic mods stay off them, and nothing changes in Penumbra's settings.
- If you gave an NPC its own collection in Penumbra, it keeps that one.
- Each PC uses its own skeletons, so a friend sees your NPC with their skeleton mods. A friend without one may still see the T-pose.

**The Crystal Odyssey.**
- **Materia beside the board.** In a duel, each side's Materia now sits in its own small panel outside the board: yours on the left, your opponent's on the right, each with its icon and name. Point at one for what it does. On a narrow screen the panels sit above the board instead.
- **Bosses show their best.** A boss's signature card was simply the first card in its deck, so the Emperor showed a Morbol and the Cloud of Darkness a Moogle, with her own card in her deck. Each boss now leads with the strongest card that fits them: Garland Tiamat, the Emperor Leviathan, the Cloud of Darkness her own card, Exdeath Omega, Kefka Ultima Weapon, Ultimecia Diabolos Hollow and Kuja Necron.
- **Tougher bosses in the middle worlds.** Exdeath, Kefka, Sephiroth and Ultimecia carried weak cards (Ultros & Typhon, a Chocobo, a Tonberry) and were easier than their own world's elites. Stronger cards from their own worlds replace those, and a boss's fifth card now comes from its own world when one fits (no more Ardbert in Kefka's deck).
- **A boss's signature card waits for the boss.** You can't claim it, buy it in a pack or be dealt it before you reach that boss's world. Astos, Rude & Reno and Seifer, who carried their boss's card, now carry Chimera, Diamond Weapon and Eden's Promise.

This update needs no relay change: everyone on 0.53.0 can play with you.
