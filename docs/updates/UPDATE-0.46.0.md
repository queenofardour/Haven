# Haven 0.46.0: the Gold Saucer at home

**The arcade machines play their real games.** Click any of the six Gold Saucer arcade furnishings and choose Play:
- Cuff-a-Cur, Crystal Tower Striker and Monster Toss;
- The Moogle's Paw;
- Out on a Limb and The Finer Miner.

Haven opens the game's own window for each one. You get the real gauge, needle and claw, the game's sounds, and the result balloons over the machine (BRUTAL!!!, PULVERIZING!!!, SEIZED and so on). You stand at the machine in its pose and swing, punch or throw. The machine reacts too, with its own sounds and effects: Cuff-a-Cur's coeurl recoils, the Striker's puck flies up the tower, the claw grabs.
- **Out on a Limb and The Finer Miner** play their "Gauge Your Skill!" and "Find the Sweet Spot!" rounds with Double Down, up to six rounds, and the game's own messages ("You sense something close…").
- **The prompt reads "for 0 MGP".** Nothing is spent or won: these games run in your home only.

**Triple Triad looks and plays like the game's.** The Triple Triad Board opens the game's own match window, drawn from its layout with the game's card art:
- the board, the hands, the score cards and the turn medallion;
- the banners: START!, BLUE TURN, the rules, PLUS, SAME, COMBO and BLUE WINS;
- the match music, and the card-game pose while you play.
- **Opponents.** Choose any of the game's Triple Triad players. Each brings their own deck and rules. Or play Haven, with rules you pick.
- **Every rule plays:** All Open, Three Open, Same, Plus and Combo, Reverse, Fallen Ace, Ascension, Descension, Order, Chaos, Swap, Sudden Death and Roulette.

**Aquariums.**
- Fish swim round their tank, each on its own path at its own depth. They sat stacked in the middle in 0.45.0.
- A one-fish bowl keeps its fish in the middle, as in the game.
- Taking a fish out works again.

**Admin page.** `Haven Admin.bat` in the project folder starts it.

Client only: works with relay 0.30.0 and Haven 0.45.0 in the same gathering. Not yet tried in game.
