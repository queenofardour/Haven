# Haven 0.51.1: NPCs get their own tab

**NPCs have their own tab** on the toolbar, beside Room. Each NPC is a card with its name, Possess, Save spot, Copy my look and Design…; Rename and Remove are under its ⋯ menu. Anyone can possess an NPC; only decorators add or change them.

**A slimmer control bar** while you control an NPC: its name, who you're speaking as (click to switch between the NPC and yourself), and Let go. The keys are shown only for the first few seconds.

**Fixes from the 0.51.0 test:**
- Jumping plays the whole jump again: take-off, fall and landing, instead of a stiff hop.
- Glamourer looks work: Glamourer couldn't find Haven's NPCs at all, so neither Copy my look nor Design… changed them. NPCs are now characters Glamourer recognises.
- NPC names show above their heads all the time, like any character's, and clicking one targets it. No more "Click to target" label.

Client only: works with relay 0.34.0.
