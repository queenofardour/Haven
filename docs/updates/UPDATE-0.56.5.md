# Haven 0.56.5: NPCs really wear their mods

**An NPC's shared mods now show on it.** In 0.56.4 Haven kept an NPC's mod files but Penumbra refused to give the NPC its collection, so it kept its plain look. Penumbra now accepts it, and an NPC whose mods you shared wears them as soon as you enter the home. The same fix lets NPCs use your skeleton mods on pose balls, which never quite reached them before.

This update needs no relay change: everyone on 0.56.x can play with you.
