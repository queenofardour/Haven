# Haven 0.63.0: tilting furniture, steadier placing, and a working canvas exit

**Tilt and lean furniture.**
- The inspector has **Tilt** and **Lean** beside the turn: tilt tips a piece forward or back, lean to one side, each in degrees. **Level it** puts a piece upright again, turned as it was.
- Tilted pieces keep their lights, doors and sounds, and their collision tilts with them.
- A tilted chair, bench or bed can't be sat or lain on. The first time you tilt one, Haven says so; tick "Don't show this warning again" to stop it (Settings → Custom content → Furniture turns it back on).
- Everyone in a gathering needs Haven 0.63.0, and the relay is updated with it.

**Placing furniture is more forgiving.**
- **A copy appears exactly where the original stands**, at the same height, turn and size. Drag it out by a handle, nudge it with the arrow keys, or press F to have it follow the cursor. Handy for a row of walls.
- **A move is no longer lost if you forget to click.** A right-click, Done, or picking up another piece sets it down where it is. Only Escape or Cancel puts it back.

**Fixes.**
- **Custom homes' Exit works.** On the blank canvas the Exit stood out of reach past the platform's edge; it now stands on the platform.
- **Saved layouts can be renamed and deleted from the Room panel**, under Apply layout…, as well as from New home.
- **The Crystal Odyssey's shop** stocks at the next world's odds, as it was meant to.
- **Real-world chat inside a home** no longer opens Haven's firewall at all: the game sends chat on a connection the firewall never touches, so nothing changes for you.
