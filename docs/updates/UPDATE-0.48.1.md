# Haven 0.48.1: the arcade clocks

**Out on a Limb and The Finer Miner show their time.** The dial beside your swings counts the round down, and Double Down shows the time you had left. Before, the dial was empty and Double Down read 0:00. The clock pauses while Double Down is asked, and each new round starts with the full time.

**Monster Toss shows your shots and time.** The Gold Saucer shows them in the duty list; Haven shows them just above yours while you play: Shots Made and Time Remaining under the machine's name.

Cuff-a-Cur, the Crystal Tower Striker and The Moogle's Paw keep their windows' own time limit, as before.

Client only: works with relay 0.32.0.
