# Haven 0.64.7: the district's ambience follows the weather outside

**A home's own ambience is back, and it now follows the weather you hear outside.** Real homes play their district's outdoor ambience on the game's Ambient Sounds volume (a Shirogane house has the coast's wind and gulls), and so do Haven's homes again: 0.64.3 to 0.64.6 tried to silence it in the belief that real homes didn't have it, which was wrong, and that's all taken out.

With a weather chosen under Room → Atmosphere → Heard outside, the district's fair-weather ambience is swapped for its version of that weather, from the district's own sounds: in Shirogane the gulls give way to the coast in the rain, the wind or a thunderstorm; in Mist to its rainstorm; in the Goblet to its rain. Day and night follow the home's time. Where a district has no version of a weather (the Empyreum has no rain, Shirogane no snow), its fair-weather sound goes quiet instead. Clear the weather and the fair-weather ambience comes back at once.

Client only: the relay is unchanged.
