# Haven 0.57.2: sharing NPC mods, better

**Guests get all of an NPC's mods.** Sync plugins like Lightless keep their own shrunk or recompressed copies of some files, so a guest's Haven used to miss those (a cloned look came through without its top, for example). Haven now recognises the sync plugin's copy of each file, changed or not.

**A bar while you share.** Sharing an NPC's mods now shows a bar under the toolbar: who has them, how far each guest has got, and that you should keep sharing. Guests see their own bar while the mods arrive.

**Clearer results.** The NPCs panel now counts every guest who was there, including those who haven't answered yet.

This update needs no relay change: everyone on 0.57.x can play with you. Guests need this version to receive mods the new way.
