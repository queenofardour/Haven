# Haven 0.47.1: Triple Triad opens as the game does, and new furniture faces you

**The opening, in the game's order.** The purple background fades in first, then the board, then the rest. Both hands fly in face down, the rules show across the board, the cards turn face up one after another (the opponent's too, where an Open rule shows them), and then START!.

**START! no longer breaks up.** It's eight strips that slide in one after another. Haven showed the later strips in place before their turn, so the word appeared doubled and jumbled. Each strip now waits, hidden, until its turn. The same fix applies to anything in the game's windows that fades in partway through an animation.

**No spin when you place a card.** A played card simply lands where you put it. Only captured cards turn over.

**Names** are in the game's own lettering, as in the Gold Saucer.

**New furniture faces you.** A piece taken from the catalogue (or your own furniture, or a building piece) now starts turned toward your camera, to the nearest quarter turn, so you see its front as you place it. R still turns it. Copies keep the rotation of the piece they copy.

Client only: works with relay 0.31.0.
