# Haven 0.64.4: no real-world ambience inside a home, second try

**The zone you came from should no longer be heard inside a home.** 0.64.3 stopped only one of the game's sound slots, and Kugane's wind and gulls weren't in it. Haven now notes every sound the real zone is playing as you enter, mutes any of them while you're in the home (even one the zone starts again), and gives each its volume back when you return. The home's music, its furnishings' sounds and the weather heard outside aren't muted.

Haven also writes the sounds it found to its log on entering, so if anything still slips through, the log says what it is.

Client only: the relay is unchanged.
