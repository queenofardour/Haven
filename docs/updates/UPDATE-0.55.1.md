# Haven 0.55.1: see what you removed

**Ghosts of removed pieces.** In Remodel, point at the spot where you removed something, or at its row in the Remodel panel, and it shows up as a see-through ghost until you look away. The tooltip says "Removed", and clicking chooses it, so you can put it back.

**A shorter removed list.** Removed pieces are grouped by kind: "Pillar or beam · 12 removed" opens to list each one, with **Put these 12 back**. Pointing at one of them in the list shows that exact piece in the room.

This update needs no relay change: everyone on 0.55.x can play with you.
