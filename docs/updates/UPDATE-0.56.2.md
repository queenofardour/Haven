# Haven 0.56.2: windows go properly, tidier remodelling

**Removed windows take their light with them.** A removed window's light shaft and the patch of light it cast on the floor now go with it, and come back when you put the window back.

**Walls with their windows.** Choosing a floor's walls in Remodel now offers to remove its pillars and its windows along with them, in one step.

**Moved pieces stay in Remodel.** A piece of the house you've moved no longer shows up as furniture in Furnish. In Remodel, point at it where it stands now to choose it, then put it back or move it again.

**No company door.** The free company door to the additional chambers no longer appears when you enter a Haven home from inside real housing.

**NPC levels.** A targeted NPC's name now has a gap after its level, as a player's does.

**MakePlace light level.** A MakePlace layout's light level now sets the room's brightness, where it used to start at the brightest. Import the layout again to pick it up.

This update needs no relay change: everyone on 0.56.x can play with you.
