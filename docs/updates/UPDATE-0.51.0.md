# Haven 0.51.0: NPC fixes and Glamourer looks

**NPCs wear your Glamourer look.** Add NPC and Copy my look now save your Glamourer state with the NPC, so it wears what you see on yourself, not your base gear. **Glamourer design…** beside each NPC dresses it in any of your saved Glamourer designs. Guests with Glamourer see the look exactly; guests without it see its plain gear.

**Fixes from the first NPC test:**
- Typing in chat (the game's chat box or Chat 2) no longer walks or jumps the NPC you control.
- A jump plays its take-off once, then falls and lands, instead of jumping again and again.
- Click an NPC to target it: the target bar shows its name, and Haven labels it with its name while you point at it or have it targeted.
- While you speak as an NPC, your typing bubble shows over the NPC, on your screen and your friends'.
- Holding both mouse buttons no longer walks your character while it is held: controlling an NPC, on a pose ball or at a Gold Saucer machine.
