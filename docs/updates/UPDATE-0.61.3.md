# Haven 0.61.3: light no longer gets in the way

**You can choose things under a window's light again.** Some furnishings cast a beam of light into the room: imitation windows, skylights, stained crystal, the ceiling Phasmascapes and a few others (28 in all). Haven treated that beam as part of the piece, so it caught clicks meant for whatever stood in or behind it: under a Starry Sky Phasmascape, nothing could be chosen or used, a Triple Triad Board included. Clicks now pass through the light, both while decorating and when using things. The pieces themselves are still chosen by what you see of them.

This update is Haven only; it works with relay 0.42.2 as it is.
