# Haven 0.58.9: sharing NPC mods, smoothed out

**No more invisible host.** While you shared an NPC's mods, guests saw you faded out, and the fade could stay after the share, leaving you invisible on their screen even through a redraw and later visits. The fade is gone: your guests see you in the NPC's look for those few seconds. Anyone an earlier version left invisible shows again when they next join you.

**Guests choose.** When you share an NPC's mods, each guest is asked whether to keep them. Saying no tells you straight away, so you aren't kept waiting.

**A reminder before you share.** Send its mods to guests now asks first, and reminds you to switch Glamourer automation off for your character while you share: it puts your own look back otherwise.

**Also:** `/haven peers` lists nearby players when you're not in a gathering, if a friend doesn't show after a visit.

This update needs no relay change: everyone on 0.57.x and 0.58.x can play with you. Your guests need 0.58.9 for the prompt; older versions still fade you.
