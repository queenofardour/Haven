# Haven 0.58.0: NPCs, simpler

**Copy my look brings your mods.** Dress yourself however you like (your Glamourer look, your Penumbra mods, your Customize+ profile) and press **Copy my look** on an NPC: it becomes you, mods and all. In a gathering, your guests get the same mods through their sync plugin straight away, since they already see you in that look.

**Send its mods to guests.** For guests who weren't there when you copied the look, the Mods tab sends an NPC's mods again: you wear its look for a moment, faded out, and your own look comes back afterwards. Your Penumbra settings are never changed. To capture a look you've never worn, **From a collection…** is still there.

**A tidier NPCs panel.** Name and level share a row, Possess sits beside "In the home", and the rest is on Look, Mods and Place tabs. Explanations moved into tooltips.

**Sturdier sharing.** A file that can't be read no longer freezes a share; your own look and collection are put back reliably, even if you leave the home mid-share; guests keep an NPC's mods only when all of them arrive (part of a set could draw a broken NPC); and old mod files are tidied away.

This update needs no relay change: everyone on 0.57.x can play with you. Guests need this version to receive mods the new way.
