# Haven 0.58.1: easier furnishing

**Pick what you point at.** Furnishings are now chosen by the shape under your mouse, as in the game's own housing, instead of a box around each model. Those boxes could be far bigger than the piece, so in a small room a shelf or partition swallowed everything near it. Now the outline and your click land on the piece you're pointing at, even when small pieces sit close together or on top of each other.

**Free camera.** While decorating, the new four-arrow button on the toolbar frees the camera from your character. **WASD** or a **middle-drag** moves it, the **wheel** zooms and a **right-drag** turns it, and it can pull back through a wall to look into a small room. Your character stays where it is. It works while you place a piece too: the piece holds still while you turn, and a plain right-click still cancels. A piece you move with the arrow keys, Page Up/Page Down or the inspector's fields takes the camera along. Click the button again (or Done) to follow your character.

**NPCs panel:** the Level field lines up with Name, and each NPC's row shows how many mods it carries.

This update needs no relay change: everyone on 0.57.x and 0.58.0 can play with you.
